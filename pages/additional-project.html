Game Overview

LiLeaps was an entry for the Juniper Game Jam which spanned for 1 week. The theme was Spin to Win, at the time for some reason I was thinking about flappy bird and frogs, so I devised a concept where the frog jumps from lily pads, to incorporate the theme I made the pads spin. Now the player must strategically, leap and land on these pads. To make sure that the player does not just camp on the pad, overtime the lily pads also shrink, so it gives the player a sense of urgency otherwise they sink. I made it highscore-based, so the player can just come back to beat their previous record for replayability. Figured, I should also add a simple combo track which works by starting a timer when the player lands and they must immediately leap again to reset and retain that timer, take too long then the combo breaks. Additional features and fixes were done after the jam, and make the game for free on Android.
